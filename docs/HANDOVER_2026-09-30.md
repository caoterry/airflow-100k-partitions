## Lessons / catches

- `inlet_events[asset]` is unbounded (no pagination) — a 10k-event pull exceeds the 5 s Task SDK timeout; page with `.after().limit()`.
- The state-store accessor is scoped to a task's declared inlets/outlets (`KeyError` otherwise).
- A batcher is a stateful service: pending / inflight / processed must be a real ledger (locked table); a KV get→set races.
- `[-1]` on `inlet_events` is registration order, not business version.
- Keyed asset events never queue non-partitioned consumers.
